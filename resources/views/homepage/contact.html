Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about ClickNCart

How do I create a seller account on ClickNCart?

Creating a seller account is simple! Click on the "Start Selling" button at the top of the page, fill out the registration form with your business details, and verify your email address. Once approved, you can set up your shop and start listing products.

What fees does ClickNCart charge sellers?

ClickNCart offers competitive pricing with a small commission on each sale. We have different plans available: Basic (5% commission), Plus (3.5% commission with additional features), and Pro (2.5% commission with all features included). There are no setup fees or monthly charges for the Basic plan.

How can buyers contact sellers?

Each product page has a "Contact Seller" button that allows buyers to send messages directly to the seller. All communication goes through our secure messaging system to protect both parties' privacy.

What payment methods does ClickNCart accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfers. Sellers receive payments directly to their registered bank account after order fulfillment.

How does ClickNCart handle returns and refunds?

Our return policy requires sellers to clearly state their return conditions on each product page. ClickNCart mediates in case of disputes and ensures fair resolution for both parties. Buyers can initiate returns through their order history page within the specified timeframe.
